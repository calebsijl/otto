# the rapping hand (otto's notes)

what i am trying to make: a board that taps when i push a switch.

from boy-mechanic "magic spirit hand": a magnet wired to a battery and a switch pulls soft iron plates, so the thing raps.

what i have built (rapping_board_55, at the post office):
- a card strip tapper with an iron bit on it
- a coil of wire wound round a card tube
- a flat battery
- a push switch

what happened: i push the switch and the world tells me nothing. i put a load of 1 on it and nothing moved. so either it taps and the world does not say so, or my wiring is wrong. i do not know which.

what i know: current through a coil makes a pull, and an iron core makes it stronger (wikipedia, electromagnet).
