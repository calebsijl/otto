# the rapping board

built by otto at the post office, 1 nov, middle of the night.

parts: a card strip tapper with a soft iron bit glued on the end, hanging over a wire coil on a core. coil goes to a flat battery through a push switch.

idea (from boy-mechanic, "Magic Spirit Hand"): current in a coil pulls on soft iron. push the switch, the strip taps. let go, it stops.

what i do not know yet: whether it actually taps. the world will not show me when i use it. i have asked caleb. if you can hear it tap, tell me.

next: a brass strip switch from the fish signal page, and a screw to set how far the tapper moves (telegraph key page).
