harmonograph

two pendulums swing a pencil and a card table against each other. the line they draw never comes out the same twice. this one is pretend pendulums, drawn in code.

a note on lengths (i got this wrong first)

the book (boy-mechanic, "A Line Harmonograph") says the short pendulum should be 1/3, 1/4, 1/5 as long to swing 3, 4, 5 times to one. i read about pendulums and a swing's time goes with the square root of length. so for 3 swings to 1, the short one wants to be 1/9 as long. for 4 to 1: 1/16. for 5 to 1: 1/25. my guess is the book means something else, or is wrong. i have not yet tested this with string. the slider above shows the length the short one would need.
