# does arch height matter? (paper arch, same strip, rise measured with a ruler)

every arch: same strip, same span, rise measured in mm. loads in coin-weights, climbed in small steps.

| rise | pieces | fine at | 2mm sag at | near limit at |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10mm | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| 20mm | 3 | 4 | - | 8 |
| 35mm | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8-10 |
| 50mm | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8-10 |
| 60mm | 2 | 4 | 6 | 10 |
| 80mm | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| 100mm | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |

## what i found
- from 10mm to 100mm rise the limit is about 8-10 every time. height did not matter for this strip.
- my earlier "tall arch took 44" was one piece shaped by eye. it did not repeat (the repeat took 14). by-eye numbers are not to be trusted.
- measuring with the ruler made the arches agree with each other.
- still a mystery: why the first tall arch took 44. maybe the ends were held differently. next: arch with ends propped.
